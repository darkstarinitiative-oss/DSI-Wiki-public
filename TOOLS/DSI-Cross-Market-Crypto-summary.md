The DSI-Cross-Market-Crypto project addresses crypto asset price discrepancies across exchanges by creating a platform for real-time/historical data ingestion, parity gap detection, strategy backtesting, and opportunity notification. Its architecture includes pluggable exchange connectors, a parity engine, simulation engine, strategy host, notification layer, and execution layer. The project is structured into milestones from M0 (governance/contracts) to M8 (monitoring UI), with current progress indicating M0 completion (interface/schemas defined in SCOPE.md).
